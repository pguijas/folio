# Code of Conduct

Folio follows a simple standard for project spaces: be respectful, assume good intent, and focus disagreement on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be direct and constructive when reviewing ideas or code.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping reports and pull requests focused.
- Accept feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner contact listed on GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other action needed to keep the project productive.
