Prototype · variant

Epic swimlanes

The columns still mean status. The rows now mean decomposition: one lane per epic, its children sitting in the column their own status says they belong in.

What this variant argues

Thesis

Decomposition and status are not two views, they are two axes of one grid. Keep the kanban columns, give the rows a meaning, and a parent becomes a horizontal lane spanning every column with its children placed by their own status. Nothing has to be opened to be seen.

The cross-column child

It resolves natively, and that is this layout's strongest argument. theme-contract-for-plugin-surfaces sits in the Backlog cell of the Plugin system unification lane while its parent is In review: one row, two columns, nothing to reconcile. The epic keeps no card in the grid, because it is the rail, so the cell it would have occupied carries an accent rule and the word EPIC instead. The distance between a parent and its children becomes a thing you read across rather than something the layout has to apologise for.

Tree filtering

The lane stays as context; the cards filter. A lane survives if the epic or any descendant matches, and inside a surviving lane only matching cards are drawn; non-matching siblings are removed, not dimmed, so the cells hold hits only. When the epic itself did not match, its rail is marked CONTEXT and greyed, so you always know whether the row title is a result or a place. Children never rise to the root: a card with no lane is a card with no story.

Progress

Each lane rail carries a four-segment bar in column order, one segment per status, sized by how many descendants sit there, plus a 0/10 count of descendants that have reached the last column. Staging a move feeds it live, so pushing work rightwards fills the lane while you watch.

What this is bad at

Depth. The lane axis encodes exactly one level of decomposition. A grandchild such as the-dialog-renders-markdown, under the-card-dialog-reads-like-a-mail, under kanban-single-board-with-filters, cannot get its own lane without shredding the board, so it falls back to indentation inside a cell — one step per level, with a mono breadcrumb when it lands in a different column than its parent. Level two reads well. By level four the indent is doing all the work inside a 208px column and the lane title has stopped describing what is under it.

Air. Six lanes by four columns is twenty-four cells and this board fills seven of them; In progress and Released are empty top to bottom, so most of the grid is whitespace and the page runs about 4,400px tall at 1440. A flat column of cards is far denser. Collapse all is the answer, and it should probably be the default.

WIP limits get blurry. The limit is a property of the column, but the eye now counts per cell. The header keeps the real column total (13/3 in red) because that is the number the limit is about, and no cell count is ever compared to it.
