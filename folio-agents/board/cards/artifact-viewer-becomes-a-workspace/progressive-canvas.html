Every step opens forward.

Responsive placement

On a very wide viewport, all four surfaces form one horizontal sequence. On an ordinary laptop, the artifact continues below the canvas and card. On a narrower viewport, card and artifact both continue downward. DOM order never changes, so keyboard and reading order remain progressive.

Unwinding

Closing the artifact returns to the card. Closing the card also closes its artifact because the child cannot outlive its context. Filters are independent. Full screen temporarily focuses the final surface and restores the complete chain on exit.
