Reading overlay

Clicking an artifact opens a reading layer over the whole canvas. The board stays mounted behind it, dimmed at the edges; Esc puts it back exactly as you left it, one level at a time.

What this variant argues

Thesis

Reading is a mode you enter deliberately: the artifact takes the viewport, prose gets a real measure, a prototype gets real room — and one keystroke returns the canvas exactly as you left it, column, dialog, filter and all.

If this wins, the docs page

The board URL becomes the reading address — #card=…&artifact=… reopens board, card and overlay. A deep link no longer needs the compiled /docs/kanban/… page as a destination; it survives only as the raw target behind “source”, and could stop being a docs-tree page at all (the sibling note on where card pages publish decides what replaces it).

What it is bad at

It is modal. You cannot read and scan the canvas at once: the board behind the overlay is a dimmed rim, context but never interaction, and comparing two artifacts means closing one to open the other. Two stacked layers of Esc is state you carry in your head. And once you click into an embedded prototype, keys go to its document — Esc only works from the overlay's own chrome.

Keyboard: Tab walks cards and artifacts, Enter opens, ←→ walk the artifact band while reading, Esc unwinds one level — reader to card, card to board.

Throwaway prototype. Static page, no network, no build step. Data is board-data.js + reader-data.js.
